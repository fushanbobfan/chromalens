Checks the WCAG 2.x contrast ratio between a foreground and background color — legibility for anyone, independent of any simulated deficiency above. A color pair can score well here and still be a poor pick under a deficiency, or the reverse, since contrast ratio measures luminance while the confusion scores above measure hue and saturation.
